How to Safely Connect and Use a Gas Cylinder

This sheet is for kitchen staff and home users in Sukuta when connecting a new LPG cylinder. Read it every time you change the cylinder.

What you need

  • 1 full LPG cylinder - 12 kg
  • Regulator and hose in good condition
  • Soapy water for leak test
  • PPE - gloves
  • Match or lighter - keep away until tested

Steps

  1. Turn the gas off at the old cylinder before disconnecting the hose.
  2. Remove regulator from old cylinder and check rubber seal.
  3. Place new cylinder upright on flat ground, not near fire.
  4. Fix regulator firmly - you must hear a click.
  5. Open cylinder valve slowly, then test joints with soapy water.
  6. If you see bubbles, close valve immediately and refix.
  7. Use 2 tbsp of soapy water - not fire - to check for leaks.
  8. Light burner only after no leak is found.

If something goes wrong

If you smell gas strongly after closing the valve, do not switch on any electrical appliance. Open all windows and doors, leave the kitchen, and call the supplier. Do not try to fix a leaking cylinder with tape. If flame is yellow and smoky, close the cylinder and clean the burner.
